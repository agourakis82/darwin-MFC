# Prior-Art Frontier v0.7

Search snapshot: 2026-08-02. This is an adversarial literature map, not a
completed novelty search, freedom-to-operate analysis, or patent opinion.

| Neighborhood | Direct prior art | Collision | Boundary retained in v0.7 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dynamic information-flow policy | [Dynamic Release](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.08096) generalizes declassification, erasure, delegation, and revocation. | Dynamic revocation and post-update noninterference are not new. | v0.7 asks whether a previously derived normative result remains identifiable after its evidence loses authority. |
| Authorization and delegation | [FLAFOL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.10630), [FLAM](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/andru/papers/flam/), and recent compositional agent-governance work model authority, delegation, and information flow. | No claim to invent authority-aware computation or delegation. | The executable treats authority snapshots as inputs and never grants permission. |
| Proof-carrying authorization with revocation | [LPCFS](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~dg/papers/stm11-lpcfs.pdf) attaches proofs to authorization and rejects proofs using revoked policy certificates. | Proof-bearing authorization and revocable credentials are established prior art. | v0.7 neither authorizes access nor validates a credential chain; it asks whether a derived normative label remains identifiable after the admissible observation set contracts. |
| Proof-carrying governed actions | [Proof-Carrying Agent Actions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04104) binds authority, approval, runtime facts, and outcome closure into portable action certificates. | No claim to invent proof-carrying decisions, actions, receipts, or runtime governance. | The v0.7 receipt carries evidence about a bounded theorem and executable, never permission to act. |
| Formal deletion | [Formalizing Data Deletion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10635) and [deletion-as-control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.07876) define post-deletion guarantees for interactive systems. | No claim to invent deletion, unlearning, or history independence. | The source may remain retained for audit; v0.7 constrains its normative influence on a decision. |
| Certified unlearning and residual influence | [Certified Data Removal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07640) and [Certified Unlearning in Decentralized Federated Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06436) explicitly seek to eliminate residual training-data influence. | Neither "residual influence" nor a certificate of its removal is new terminology or a new objective. | v0.7 is not statistical unlearning: the retained data may remain intact, while an exact authority projection constrains only legitimate decision emission. |
| Counterfactual invariance | [Results on Counterfactual Invariance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08519) and earlier work analyze outputs invariant to interventions. | Output invariance under changing inputs is established prior art. | The intervention set is generated by a versioned authority revocation, and failure has a zero-error abstention consequence. |
| Provenance and deletion propagation | [Provenance Semirings](https://www.cs.ucdavis.edu/~green/papers/pods07.pdf), [provenance as dependency analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.2173), and classical view deletion study derivation witnesses and minimal deletion sets. | Minimal supports, lineage, and deletion cuts are not new. | v0.7 uses exact support/cut search only as a bounded witness around normative identifiability. |
| Governed-output correction workflows | [US 2026/0121859 A1](https://patents.justia.com/patent/20260121859) describes governed evidence updates, impact analysis, deterministic replay, output revocation, and superseding views. | Direct collision with broad claims to correction propagation or revalidation of an evidence-bound output. | v0.7 does not claim the workflow. It seeks a necessary mathematical survival condition and a zero-error abstention consequence. |
| Truth maintenance and belief contraction | d-DNNF truth maintenance supports assertion and retraction; dynamic epistemic logic and AGM-style work model belief contraction. | Retraction of beliefs and consequences is not new. | Required recommendations are uninterpreted external labels; the theorem is about legitimate decision emission, not belief revision. |
| Living clinical guidance | Living-guideline methods treat the individual recommendation as an update unit and define surveillance triggers; recommendation reversals are empirically documented. | Updating or retiring clinical recommendations is established practice. | v0.7 supplies no clinical update rule; it studies a formal condition for survival versus abstention. |

## Candidate Contribution

The surviving hypothesis is the conjunction of:

1. a distinction between retained evidence and currently authorized evidence;
2. an end-to-end no-residual-influence property for derived normative outputs;
3. a zero-error forced-abstention theorem after authority contraction;
4. separate certificates for survival support, residual restore, and
   revocation fragility;
5. source-fresh Sounio native/WASM execution with an exhaustive bounded oracle.

No component is presumed novel. The conjunction remains only a candidate until
academic and patent searches close claim by claim.

The patent collision above materially narrows the candidate. Any future claim
must exclude evidence-update orchestration, deterministic replay, token
revocation, superseding views, and generic impact analysis.
