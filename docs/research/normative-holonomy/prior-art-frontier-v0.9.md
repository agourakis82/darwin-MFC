# Prior-art frontier v0.9

## Known neighboring results

The following components are established research areas and are excluded from
any novelty claim:

1. Sheaf-theoretic contextuality and obstruction by absence of global
   sections.
2. Composition, closure, consistency, and path reasoning in networks of
   ontology alignments.
3. Holonomy, monodromy, signed-graph balance, and fixed-point obstructions.
4. Impossible or conflicting obligations in deontic logic.
5. Executable clinical guidelines, FHIR computable-guideline workflows, and
   proof-carrying or provenance-bearing decisions.

## Surviving candidate conjunction

The candidate contribution is intentionally narrower:

> A content-addressed network of locally proof-carrying normative
> translations whose cycle composition is checked for witness-preserving
> holonomy, with a formally proved and source-fresh executable obligation to
> abstain when no global witness exists.

The scientific object is not merely inconsistency. It is a measurable failure
of witness transport around a certified cycle, accompanied by a minimal
obstruction certificate and a no-label executable result.

## Falsifiers

The novelty hypothesis is defeated by prior work that already combines all of
the following in one operative construction:

- normative rather than merely descriptive transports;
- local proof objects bound to content-addressed translations;
- cycle-level fixed-point or global-section checking;
- a minimal machine-verifiable obstruction or repair cut;
- an executable abstention theorem under a zero-error emission contract;
- artifact identity linking source, proof, compiler, native result, and WASM.

Finding any one component is not a falsifier because the components are known.
Exact-phrase absence is weak negative evidence and will never be treated as a
novelty proof.

## Search protocol

Formal novelty review must include backward and forward citation chasing across
computer science, mathematical logic, knowledge representation, clinical
informatics, safety engineering, and patent literature. Queries must include
synonyms for global section, cocycle, monodromy, path independence, alignment
network, deontic conflict, executable guideline, abstention, and proof-carrying
decision. Results are recorded claim by claim; `noveltyEstablished` remains
`false` until independent review closes that matrix.
