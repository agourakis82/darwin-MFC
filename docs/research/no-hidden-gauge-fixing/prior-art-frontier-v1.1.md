# Prior-art frontier v1.1

## Known ingredients

The following families are adjacent and prevent broad novelty claims:

- symmetry-breaking and deterministic leader-election impossibility in
  anonymous systems;
- group actions, stabilizers, equivariant maps, and fixed-point obstructions;
- permutation-group bases as sets of points whose pointwise stabilizer is
  trivial;
- mathematical structuralism and the problem of choosing representatives in
  the presence of automorphisms;
- set-valued treatment recommendations, partial identification, selective
  prediction, and clinical abstention;
- provenance graphs, executable guidelines, assurance cases, and
  proof-carrying software.

## Narrow candidate conjunction

The current research candidate is not the stabilizer theorem itself. It is the
following conjunction:

1. normative alternatives are represented as a gauge action rather than as
   unexplained scalar scores;
2. an executable kernel computes the exact input stabilizer and refuses a
   singular witness when no admissible fixed point exists;
3. the kernel computes a minimum external anchor set needed to destroy that
   stabilizer;
4. any later singular output must carry a content-addressed receipt linking the
   observation, stabilizer reduction, evidence graph, compiler, proof, and
   executable artifact;
5. source changes revoke descendant results instead of silently preserving a
   previous gauge fixing.

No completed search has established that this conjunction lacks prior art.
Patentability, scientific originality, usefulness, and regulatory acceptability
are distinct questions.

## Formal search protocol still required

A publishable novelty position requires dated searches in PubMed, Crossref,
IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, arXiv, WIPO Patentscope, Espacenet, Google
Patents, and INPI. Queries must include synonyms for equivariant selection,
stabilizer certificates, external symmetry breaking, provenance-bound choice,
executable clinical constraints, and revocable normative decisions. Every
candidate claim must be mapped against each reference, limitation by
limitation, and reviewed independently.
