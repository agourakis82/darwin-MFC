# Preregistration Protocol: Deontic Transportability of Clinical Recommendations

Protocol version: 0.1

Date: 2026-08-02

Registration status: **DRAFT, NOT YET REGISTERED**

Analysis status: **NO CONFIRMATORY DATA ANALYZED**

Clinical disposition: **REFUSE**

## 1. Scientific objective

The program tests whether transporting clinical effects is sufficient to
transport the direction, strength, and scope of a recommendation. It combines:

1. a paired retrospective study of source and officially adapted guidelines;
2. a randomized factorial experiment using synthetic Evidence-to-Decision
   packets; and
3. a formal partial-identification analysis over admissible normative
   contexts.

The retrospective study establishes prevalence and taxonomy, not causality.
The randomized study tests causal effects of contextual decision factors while
holding clinical evidence fixed. The formal study tests whether observed panel
choices fall inside a preregistered recommendation identified set.

## 2. Primary questions

**RQ1.** Among recommendation pairs with an identical decision-relevant causal
evidence packet, how often do direction or strength differ after formal
adaptation to a target context?

**RQ2.** Holding a synthetic causal evidence packet fixed, do randomized
changes in values/preferences, resource feasibility, and equity information
change recommendation direction, strength, or abstention?

**RQ3.** Does adding target normative information contract the formal
recommendation identified set, and when does a nonempty robust core emerge?

## 3. Hypotheses

### Confirmatory hypotheses

- `H1`: at least one traceable pair will exhibit a direction or strength change
  with an identical decision-relevant evidence packet and an explicitly
  documented noncausal EtD driver.
- `H2-resource`: randomized resource/feasibility constraints will change the
  distribution of direction, strength, or abstention relative to the same
  evidence packet without those constraints.
- `H2-values`: randomized heterogeneity in patient values/preferences will
  change the distribution of recommendation strength or abstention.
- `H2-equity`: randomized adverse equity impact will change the distribution
  of direction, strength, or abstention.
- `H3`: revealing all prespecified target normative domains will weakly reduce
  the size of the recommendation identified set compared with revealing causal
  evidence alone.

`H1` is deliberately existential because the formal insufficiency claim is
falsified by a universal sufficiency claim, not by a prevalence threshold. The
study will estimate prevalence, but no arbitrary minimum prevalence is used as
the definition of the phenomenon.

### Falsification outcomes

- No eligible pair satisfies `H1` after independent adjudication.
- Context manipulations fail their checks or produce no detectable movement in
  any preregistered recommendation outcome.
- Identified-set predictions systematically exclude adjudicated panel
  recommendations.
- Evidence-lineage and driver coding cannot reach the reliability gate.

## 4. Study A: paired guideline adaptation atlas

### 4.1 Unit of analysis

The unit is one source/adapted recommendation pair addressing the same clinical
question and linked by an official adaptation process. A guideline document is
not the unit.

### 4.2 Eligible sources

An adaptation program is eligible only when all of the following are public:

- source recommendation and target recommendation;
- direction and strength, or enough official metadata to encode them;
- source and target PICO or an equivalent structured question;
- evidence-review lineage and update date;
- an adaptation method or EtD process; and
- rationale or judgments for changed recommendations.

Candidate frameworks include GRADE-ADOLOPMENT and other formal methods, but the
method name alone does not establish eligibility.

### 4.3 Exclusions

- informal translations without a target panel decision;
- different PICOs that cannot be reconciled;
- summaries with no traceable full recommendation pair;
- adaptations that silently update evidence without an auditable search date;
- recommendations generated by the Darwin application; and
- patient-level or proprietary clinical data.

### 4.4 Feasibility gate and corpus lock

A systematic search and screening flow will be frozen before extraction. The
confirmatory atlas requires at least 100 eligible pairs from at least three
independent adaptation programs and three clinical domains. If this gate is
not met, the work will be labeled a feasibility study and only descriptive
results will be reported.

The current ten-row ASH metadata atlas is a discovery seed and is excluded from
confirmatory analysis until the original/adapted recommendation texts and
evidence packets are independently extracted and frozen.

### 4.5 Evidence-lineage classification

Two evidence reviewers, blinded to the final adapted recommendation whenever
possible, independently assign one category:

```text
E0_IDENTICAL_DECISION_PACKET
E1_UPDATED_NO_DECISION_RELEVANT_CHANGE
E2_TARGET_EFFECT_TRANSPORT_SUPPORTED
E3_DECISION_RELEVANT_CAUSAL_CHANGE
E4_PICO_CHANGED
E5_NOT_ADJUDICABLE
```

`E0` requires the same outcome estimates, certainty judgments, evidence search
cutoff, and decision thresholds. `E1` requires a prospectively defined
outcome-specific equivalence or decision threshold; statistical
non-significance is not sufficient. `E2` requires explicit transport
assumptions and target evidence. A citation carried forward is not by itself a
transportability proof.

The primary `H1` denominator uses only `E0`. `E1` and `E2` enter prespecified
secondary analyses. `E3-E5` never support a deontic transport-gap claim.

### 4.6 Recommendation encoding

For source and target, reviewers encode independently:

- direction: `FOR`, `AGAINST`, `NO_RECOMMENDATION`, `MIXED`, `UNCLEAR`;
- strength in the native scheme;
- a reviewed crosswalk, if any, to `STRONG`, `CONDITIONAL`, or `UNMAPPED`;
- scope: population, intervention, comparator, setting, jurisdiction, and
  exceptions;
- represented constituency and issuing authority; and
- exact source span and version.

Strength is never inferred from modal verbs alone. Cross-scheme comparisons are
excluded unless two methodologists approve the mapping before seeing the study
outcome.

### 4.7 Adaptation-driver encoding

Drivers are multilabel:

```text
CAUSAL_EVIDENCE
BASELINE_RISK
VALUES_PREFERENCES
RESOURCE_USE
COST_EFFECTIVENESS
ACCESS_FEASIBILITY
EQUITY
ACCEPTABILITY
LEGAL_REGULATORY
AUTHORITY_CONSTITUENCY
IMPLEMENTATION
LANGUAGE_FORCE_MAPPING
UNCLEAR_OTHER
```

Every driver requires an exact locator. Reviewers distinguish a panel's stated
rationale from their own inferred explanation.

### 4.8 Independence and adjudication

- Two evidence reviewers adjudicate evidence lineage.
- Two guideline-methodology reviewers encode recommendation properties and
  drivers.
- A fifth reviewer adjudicates unresolved disagreements.
- No person may adjudicate their own extraction.
- Conflicts of interest and prior authorship of a source guideline are recorded.

The evidence-lineage team is separated from the recommendation-change team
until both first-pass encodings are locked.

### 4.9 Outcomes

Primary outcome:

```text
DTG_any_E0 = proportion of E0 pairs with a changed direction or mapped strength
             and at least one explicit noncausal EtD driver.
```

Secondary outcomes:

- direction retention;
- strength retention within compatible schemes;
- scope retention;
- driver-specific change proportions;
- `DTG_any` under `E0+E1` and `E0+E1+E2`;
- frequency of `UNMAPPED`, `UNCLEAR`, and `NOT_ADJUDICABLE`; and
- robust-core coverage after formal encoding.

### 4.10 Reliability gate

Before adjudication, report raw agreement and Krippendorff's alpha for evidence
lineage, direction, mapped strength, and each driver. Alpha below `0.67` blocks
confirmatory interpretation. Values from `0.67` to below `0.80` are labeled
tentative; `0.80` or above passes the planned reliability gate. Confidence
intervals are reported and no disagreement is deleted.

### 4.11 Statistical analysis

- Report counts, denominators, and Wilson 95% intervals for proportions.
- The primary analysis is descriptive and pair-level.
- Secondary models use hierarchical multinomial or ordinal regression with
  random intercepts for adaptation program and clinical domain.
- No p-value converts a retrospective driver association into causality.
- Missing driver data are not imputed; sensitivity bounds classify all missing
  cases first as changed and then as unchanged.
- All exclusions and denominator changes are shown in a flow diagram.

## 5. Study B: randomized normative-context experiment

### 5.1 Design

A repeated-measures randomized factorial experiment presents synthetic EtD
packets. Each base clinical question has a fixed evidence table. Participants
receive randomized target-context modules while the evidence table, PICO,
wording template, and display order remain invariant.

The core factors are:

- resources/feasibility: available versus materially constrained;
- values/preferences: concentrated versus heterogeneous;
- equity: neutral versus distributionally adverse; and
- target baseline implementation context: aligned versus misaligned.

A separate block varies the decision authority/constituency between a
population guideline panel and an individual shared-decision setting. Results
from these authority conditions are not pooled as though they answered the same
question.

### 5.2 Participants

Recruit four strata:

- guideline methodologists;
- practicing clinicians;
- health economists or implementation scientists; and
- patient/public representatives trained for the task.

Credential and experience criteria will be frozen before recruitment. No
participant will make a decision for a real patient.

### 5.3 Outcomes

After each packet, participants select:

- direction;
- strength in one standardized experimental scheme;
- `ABSTAIN / INSUFFICIENT CONTEXT` when appropriate;
- confidence;
- decisive EtD domains; and
- a short rationale used only for qualitative mechanism analysis.

The primary endpoint is the joint categorical outcome
`direction x strength x abstention`. Secondary endpoints include response time,
confidence calibration, and comprehension of which facts were held constant.

### 5.4 Manipulation checks

Participants must correctly identify that the clinical evidence did not change
and identify the randomized context domain. Failed checks are retained in the
intention-to-treat analysis and excluded only in a prespecified per-protocol
sensitivity analysis.

### 5.5 Randomization and masking

- Context conditions are computer-randomized within base question.
- A balanced incomplete block prevents any participant from seeing all variants
  of one base question.
- Order is randomized and immediate variant pairs are prohibited.
- Analysts receive masked factor labels until data cleaning and exclusions are
  frozen.

### 5.6 Sample size

A pilot estimates within-participant correlation, category sparsity, and
manipulation-check failure. The confirmatory sample size will then be computed
for the smallest substantively meaningful shift in the joint outcome and
frozen in a new protocol version before confirmatory recruitment. Version 0.1
does not invent a sample size without those inputs.

### 5.7 Analysis

- Intention-to-treat mixed-effects multinomial regression is primary.
- Prespecified fixed effects are the four context factors and their two-way
  interactions; participant and base question receive random intercepts.
- Authority/constituency is analyzed in its separate block.
- Multiplicity is controlled across the three confirmatory domain contrasts.
- Report absolute predicted-probability changes, not only odds ratios.
- Qualitative rationales test mechanism concordance but do not replace the
  randomized estimate.

## 6. Study C: formal prediction test

Before unmasking each target decision, formal encoders declare:

- the causal evidence set `E_T`;
- the admissible normative context set `Theta_T`;
- the predicted recommendation identified set `R_ID(T)`;
- the robust action core `C_T`; and
- assumptions whose violation invalidates the result.

Primary formal metrics:

- **set coverage**: fraction of adjudicated recommendations contained in the
  preregistered identified set;
- **sharpness**: normalized size of the identified set;
- **robust-core yield**: fraction with a nonempty robust core; and
- **false universality**: fraction where source recommendation was treated as
  unique but the target recommendation falls outside it while inside the
  broader identified set.

A vacuous set that contains every recommendation can achieve coverage but will
fail sharpness. A narrow set that excludes target decisions will fail coverage.

## 7. Data governance

- Only public guideline documents, reviewer codes, and synthetic experiment
  materials are used in the first phase.
- No patient record, prescription, dose, or identifiable health information is
  collected.
- Reviewer identities are pseudonymized in public releases.
- Source documents are stored only where licensing permits; otherwise the
  release contains URLs, locators, hashes, and extraction metadata.
- Every protocol, codebook, data freeze, and analysis release receives a
  content-addressed receipt.

Ethics review or a formal determination of exemption will be obtained before
human-participant recruitment. Public-document extraction alone does not waive
review for the prospective experiment.

## 8. Registration and change control

Before confirmatory work:

1. complete the systematic prior-art review;
2. register the protocol and analysis plan on OSF or an equivalent registry;
3. freeze inclusion criteria, codebook, evidence thresholds, and crosswalks;
4. publish hashes for the protocol and analysis container;
5. label every later change as prospective amendment or post hoc analysis; and
6. preserve negative and null results.

The ASH discovery atlas may guide instrument design but cannot be silently
promoted into the confirmatory dataset.

## 9. Stop conditions

The program pauses before manuscript claims if:

- exact prior art is found;
- the reliability gate fails after one documented codebook revision;
- fewer than 100 eligible pairs or fewer than three independent programs exist;
- source/adapted evidence lineage cannot be reconstructed;
- prospective manipulation checks fail materially; or
- the formal model obtains apparent performance only through outcome-informed
  expansion of `Theta_T`.
