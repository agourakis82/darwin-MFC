## Preliminary reading

The combination cannot currently be called pioneering. Executable clinical guidelines, medication-rule authoring, deterministic prescribing governance, formal methods, provenance, and medical-device assurance cases all have substantial prior art. The candidate contribution, if any, must survive claim-level analysis of the specific composition: living medication evidence graph, exact-dimensional constraint compiler, independently checked proof, source/compiler/WASM identity, per-action counterexample, scoped credential, and local-only patient context.
